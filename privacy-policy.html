MacroCiv Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 App: MacroCiv (Android, package com.macrociv.app) Developer: Fieldnote App Studio Contact: fieldnoteappstudio@gmail.com

Summary

MacroCiv does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The app has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts and no sign-in.

Information we collect

None. MacroCiv does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers or any other personal or device information. It requests only one Android permission, Internet access, which is used only as described below.

Network use

All political, economic-freedom, civil-liberties and election data is built into the app, so it works fully offline.

When the device is online, the app checks for an updated copy of the same public data, at most once every 6 hours, or when you tap "Data updated". To do this it downloads two public files over HTTPS from raw.githubusercontent.com, a file-hosting service run by GitHub, Inc.:

  • a small version file, and
  • if a newer version exists, the data file itself.

These requests contain no personal information and no identifiers created by MacroCiv. As with any web request, GitHub's servers receive standard connection information such as your IP address, the time of the request and basic request headers, and may log it under GitHub's own privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Fieldnote App Studio does not receive or have access to those logs.

Data stored on your device

Downloaded data files and the time of the last update check are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They contain only public data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes them.

Third parties

MacroCiv contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or social SDKs. Data is not shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

MacroCiv is a general-audience educational app and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Update downloads use HTTPS. Each downloaded data file is checked against its published SHA-256 checksum and validated before the app uses it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
